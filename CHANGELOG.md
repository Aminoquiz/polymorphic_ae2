## [1.0.0] - 2026.08.26

First release. Brings Polymorph+ recipe conflict picking to Applied Energistics 2.

### Added

- **Crafting terminal.** When the ingredients you put in match more than one recipe, the
  Polymorph+ button appears and you choose which result you want, exactly as you would at a
  crafting table.
- **Pattern encoding terminal.** Same thing when encoding a crafting pattern, so the pattern
  stores the recipe you actually meant rather than whichever one the game happened to pick.
- The picker follows the terminal when you switch between terminal layouts.
